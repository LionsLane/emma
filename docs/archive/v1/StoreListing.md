## Description (iOS 4,000 / Play 4,000)
EMMA is the marketing team that already knows your business.

Tell EMMA what you need in one sentence and she gets to work: a content plan for the week, posts and captions in your voice, follow-ups for the people who asked about your prices, and a list of leads worth calling.

Nothing goes out without you. Every plan, post and message arrives as a proposal you approve or discard.

WHAT EMMA DOES
• Plans your posts around your offers, holidays and busy seasons
• Writes captions, ads, emails and WhatsApp messages in your voice
• Keeps your leads in one place, with call, WhatsApp and email in a tap
• Reminds you who to follow up, so enquiries don't go cold
• Shows what you've planned and what's working

BUILT FOR KENYAN BUSINESSES
• Made in Nairobi by LionsLane
• Designed around WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn
• Consent flags on every lead, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act

PLANS
EMMA is free to start. Pro and Max plans are managed in your EMMA account.

Your workspace is private to your account. EMMA's responses are generated by AI and can be wrong: review everything before you publish it.
