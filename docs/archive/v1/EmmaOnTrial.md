# EMMA on trial

> **Pricing superseded (2026-10-09):** the real plans are Free, Pro KES 500 and Max KES 1,500 (see PRD.md). The KES 25,000 / 15,000 figures below were placeholders the jury needed to judge the pitch.

EMMA's own pitch, put in front of the Pitch Jury and fixed until all nine jurors acquitted it. Open **Pitch Jury → EMMA by LionsLane** in the app to read every juror's verdict, reasoning and fix.

> These verdicts came from Claude playing each juror, which is exactly what the feature does live. Treat them as a stress test of the pitch, not market research. Real buyers get the final vote.

## The rounds

| Round | Verdict | What moved |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **1 of 9** not guilty | The original line from the EMMA brief. No price, "any sector, any market", and "a running marketing operation… and proof it's all working" overclaimed what's built. Only the referral partner liked it. |
| 2 | **5 of 9** | Named the buyer (Kenyan SMEs on WhatsApp, Instagram and LinkedIn), added a price in shillings, approvals and a monthly LionsLane strategist. Buyers, the competitor, the marketer, the partner and the CFO flipped. The skeptic, engineer, lawyer and investor held out. |
| 3 | **9 of 9** | Led with what a chat app can't do (it already knows your business), stopped promising an automatic cost-per-lead figure, made the guarantee objective, added a data-protection line and a founding offer for the first 10 businesses. |

## The acquitted pitch

> **EMMA by LionsLane: the marketing team that already knows your business. KES 25,000 a month.**
>
> For Kenyan SMEs that sell on WhatsApp, Instagram and LinkedIn. Tell EMMA what you need in one sentence. She already knows your products, customers and calendar, so she plans the month, writes the posts, ads and emails, and finds and scores leads worth calling. Nothing goes out until you approve it, and a LionsLane strategist reviews your leads and spend with you every month.
>
> Unlike a chat app, EMMA keeps everything in one place: your calendar, campaigns, leads and results.
>
> Your data stays yours: exportable any time and processed under Kenya's Data Protection Act.
>
> Founding offer: the first 10 businesses pay KES 15,000 a month for a year in return for a case study. Cancel in the first 30 days and pay nothing.

**The prices are placeholders.** The jury needed numbers to judge, so these were set below a typical freelancer retainer. LionsLane needs to confirm them against the real cost per client (now measured in the app, see below).

## The conditions the verdict depends on

The acquittal holds only if these become true:

| Juror | Condition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Investor | Measure AI cost per client from day one, don't estimate it. | **Done.** Every EMMA and jury request records its token usage; Client Profile → *AI cost for this client* shows the monthly cost. |
| Lawyer | Show clients what leaves their browser. | **Done.** Client Profile → *What leaves this browser*. |
| Lawyer | Publish a privacy notice and sign a data processing agreement (naming Anthropic as a sub-processor) before the first contract. | **Open: LionsLane paperwork.** |
| Engineer | Move the workspace from the browser to a hosted database before onboarding ten businesses. | **Open: the next build.** |
| Skeptic | In the demo, prove the memory: ask EMMA about last month and let her answer from the client's own data. | Works today with a key ("Analyse last month"). Make it the first thing in every sales demo. |

## What the jury changed, in one line each

1. **Price and buyer up front.** "Any sector, any market" reads as "not for me".
2. **Only promise what's built.** No "running operation", no automatic cost per lead until the Meta and Google sync ships.
3. **Lead with memory and approvals, not writing.** Writing is free elsewhere; knowing the business isn't.
4. **The strategist is the wedge.** Platforms can copy features, not a Nairobi strategist on a KES 25k account.
5. **Guarantees must be objective.** "Saved you 10 hours" becomes a refund dispute; "cancel in 30 days, pay nothing" doesn't.
6. **The founding ten are the launch channel.** LionsLane's existing client book becomes the first customers and the first case studies.
